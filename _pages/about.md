杨燕鸣，武汉大学武汉数学与智能研究院助理教授、研究员，获浙江省优秀博士毕业生，浙江大学校优秀博士毕业生，浙江大学竺可桢奖学金。主要研究领域包括智能化软件工程、软件测试、代码质量保障与优化、代码生成，多项研究成果被软件工程领域的高水平期刊和会议接收和发表，其中包括中国计算机学会（CCF）A类推荐期刊和会议（如ICSE、FSE、TSE、TOSEM等），曾担任国际期刊审稿人和会议评审委员会成员，如国际期刊TOSEM、ASE、Journal of Software: Evolution and Process 和国际会议ICSE、APSEC等。Yang Yanming is an assistant professor and researcher at the Wuhan Institute of Mathematics and Intelligence, Wuhan University. He received the title of "Outstanding Doctoral Graduate of Zhejiang Province" and was an "Outstanding Doctoral Graduate of Zhejiang University" as well as a recipient of the Zhu Kezhen Scholarship from Zhejiang University. His main research areas include intelligent software engineering, software testing, code quality assurance and optimization, and code generation. Many of his research achievements have been accepted and published in high-level journals and conferences in the field of software engineering, including CCF A-class recommended journals and conferences (such as ICSE, FSE, TSE, TOSEM, etc.). He has served as a reviewer for international journals and a member of the review committee for conferences, such as TOSEM, ASE, Journal of Software: Evolution and Process, and international conferences ICSE, APSEC, etc.

My research interest includes neural machine translation and computer vision.
